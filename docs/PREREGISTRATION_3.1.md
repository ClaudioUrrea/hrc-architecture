# Pre-registration of the release-3.1 evaluation (campaigns v4)

This file is committed at the tag `r31-calibration` and published on GitHub **before any
calibration or campaign run of release 3.1**. It fixes the code, the calibration rule, the
campaign design, the hypotheses and the analysis. The frozen configuration produced by the
calibration rule is tagged `campaign-v4`. Its identifiers (commit, tree, archive and executable
hashes) are published in `docs/freeze/FREEZE_campaign-v4.md` on the main branch through the
GitHub API, before the first campaign run. GitHub's server timestamps on those objects are the
external record of the order of events. The configurations of the comparison campaign are
generated from the tuning runs by the fixed rule. They are tagged `campaign-v4-compare` and
published before the first comparison run. The results are tagged `campaign-v4-report`.

Release 3.0 and its records (tags `campaign-v3*`, Figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.34845711) are
not modified. Their evaluation is described as *pre-specified*: its design freeze is dated
only by local git metadata, and the first public commitment (`PREREGISTRATION.md`,
26 October 2026) came before the 3.0 comparison campaign only.

## 1. Code

Release 3.1 corrects the defects that Reviewer 2 identified in round 4 (`docs/CHANGES_3.1.md`).
In short:
- every speed limit is applied inside the barrier QP, and the dispatched command is checked
  against every row, the box and the SSM limit;
- the supervisor composes its limits over all active conditions;
- the seqlock is race-free;
- the cycle record carries the simulator ground truth and the estimate error;
- execution time is measured to the completion of the enqueue.

Tests: `ctest` (including `test_r31`). Executables are built with
`cmake -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release` (`-O2 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -std=gnu++17`, GCC 11.4;
`docs/BUILD.md`).

## 2. Calibration of the human-position uncertainty (rule fixed here)

The release-3.1 pilot showed that the fused human estimate of the simulator deviates from the
simulated ground truth by much more than the position uncertainty `Z_d = 0.04 m` assumed by the
SSM term. The geometric barrier contained no position-uncertainty term at all. Rule:

1. **Runs.** `cfg/campaigns/calibration`: scenarios S1–S6, 10 min each, proposed strategy, no
   faults, position margin 0, every other parameter as in `cfg/base_sim_ur5e.json`.
2. **Statistic.** The 99th percentile of `est_err_closest_m` (error of the estimated keypoint
   closest to the robot, against ground truth at the estimate's observation time), pooled over
   every matched cycle of the six runs.
3. **Margin.** `m` = that percentile rounded **up** to the next centimetre.
4. **Configuration.** `control.position_margin = m` (subtracted from every geometric barrier
   value; the potential-field baselines see the same reduced distance) and
   `ssm.Z_d = max(0.04, m)` (SSM, every strategy that uses it). No other parameter changes.

Implementation: `tools/calibrate_position_margin.py --apply`. The calibration runs are deposited
with the campaign records. If fewer than six calibration runs complete, nothing is applied and
the queue stops.

| Campaign | Runs | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ablation | 210 | 10 000 cycles each | A0–A3 as in 3.0; ground-truth probe disabled (no evaluation thread in a timing experiment) |

The seeds are the same as in 3.0. Campaign order: calibration → freeze → ablation → injection →
sensitivity → tuning → (compare generated and tagged) → compare → timing → protocol bench →
code metrics → report. Every run is mirrored to two backups and verified by hash before the
next run starts.

## 4. Hypotheses and analysis (fixed here)

- **Comparison.** For each of the four baselines (hc_cbf, ssm, pf, pf_ssm), paired over
  segments: mean task-cycle time and minimum estimated separation, proposed minus baseline. That
  gives 8 two-sided paired t-tests, Holm-adjusted together at α = 0.05, each with a BCa 95 %
  interval. A non-significant difference is **not** reported as equivalence; no equivalence
  margin is defined. Secondary endpoint, outside the Holm family: the minimum TRUE separation
  (ground truth), with paired intervals. The pf baseline runs under a different safety policy
  (no SSM limit); pf_ssm runs under the same SSM limit as the other strategies.
- **Tuning rule** (unchanged from 3.0): for each strategy, the value with the shortest mean task
  cycle among those whose estimated separation never falls below 0.15 m on any tuning segment.
- **Timing.** Execution time runs from wake-up to completion of the enqueue of the cycle record.
  Cycles carrying an injected overrun are excluded from the nominal distribution and reported
  separately. Deadline misses are judged at enqueue completion. The block-level bound treats
  each cold-started block as one independent trial.
- **Faults.** One-to-one injection–detection matching (2-s window). Ambiguous, overlapping and
  absorbed injections are reported. Episode-specific recovery. Three availability metrics
  (outside stop modes, motion permitted, mean speed scale as a proxy), time-weighted. Rates are
  given with exact Poisson intervals and with run-level bootstrap intervals and the dispersion
  index. The Weibull fit is descriptive, with a parametric-bootstrap goodness of fit.
  Simulation-campaign rates are not field reliability estimates. The sensitivity campaign is
  exploratory (one run per configuration).
- **Safety behaviour** (`analysis/behaviour.py`): largest logged violation of the dispatched
  command, and controller-level stops by cause. Floor crossings are counted separately on the
  estimated and on the true separation, with the robot state at onset (standstill /
  decelerating / moving), the supervisor mode and any preceding injected lunge. Estimate error
  is compared with `Z_d` and with the position margin.
- **Stopping.** `tools/stop_check` on the configurations and commanded speeds of the comparison
  campaign (3 rad/s² joint deceleration, `T_r` of the configuration), against Eq. (3).

Every reported number is computed by `analysis/report.py` from the raw run directories.
